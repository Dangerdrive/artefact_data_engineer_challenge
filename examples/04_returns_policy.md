# Conversation 4 — Returns policy (non-trivial: policy application)

**Scenario:** the customer regrets an online purchase and asks about returning it,
then asks how long the refund takes. This requires applying the store's policy.

**User:** I changed my mind about my purchase, can I return it?

**Lira (agent):**
> Online purchases can be returned within 7 calendar days of receipt (right of
> withdrawal), in the original packaging; the refund goes back to the original
> payment method within 10 business days and return shipping is on us. Defective
> products can be exchanged within 30 calendar days. Exchanges by preference
> (color/model/size) are allowed within 7 days, subject to availability.
> Custom-made items, 'final sale' items and wind mouthpieces cannot be exchanged.

**User:** How long does the refund take?
